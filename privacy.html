🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use SocialWiki:

  • Account Information: Email, username, password (encrypted)
  • Profile Information: Full name, avatar, bio
  • Content: Posts, comments, likes, messages
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device
  • Authentication: Google OAuth data (if you sign in with Google)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the Platform
  • Authenticate your account
  • Display personalized content
  • Show relevant advertisements
  • Calculate earnings and rewards
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you logged in
  • Remember your preferences (language, theme)
  • Analyze site traffic
  • Display targeted ads

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication
  • Google OAuth: Sign-in with Google
  • HilltopAds: Advertisement delivery
  • Cloudflare: Hosting and CDN
  • ui-avatars.com: Default profile pictures

Each service has its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing them.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers (hosting, analytics)
  • Advertisers (anonymized data only)
  • Law enforcement (when legally required)

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encrypted passwords (bcrypt)
  • HTTPS encryption
  • Row Level Security (RLS) on database
  • Regular security audits

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

8. Children's Privacy

SocialWiki is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. International Users

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. By using SocialWiki, you consent to such transfers.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@socialwiki.com
